FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

How does Detally work?

Simply type natural messages like @John owe 500$ or @Anna paid 200$. Detally automatically records and organizes everything for you.

Does Detally require an account?

No. The current G1 – Grow release works offline-first and stores your data locally on your device.

Is my data uploaded to the cloud?

Not in the current release. G1 – Grow keeps your records local for simplicity and privacy.

Who is Detally for?

Friends splitting bills, roommates, freelancers, small shops, teams, or anyone tired of messy debt notes.
